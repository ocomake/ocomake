Naoneul Privacy Policy

Effective November 28, 2026 · Applies to Naoneul 0.8.51 and later · Developer: ocomake (independent developer)

한국어

Naoneul helps you save, organize, and find notes and tasks. By default, notes, attachments, Ieum AI conversations, and analysis are stored and processed on your device. The developer does not operate a separate server to collect or store your records. External services can process some information for model downloads, link previews, third-party SDK features, and sharing or exports you start.

1. Information processed and why

  • Records and attachments. The app stores text you enter, select, capture, or share into Naoneul, as well as attachments such as photos, videos, or audio, comments, and book organization. It uses these to show, search, and organize your records.
  • Place names saved in older versions. Area names or addresses attached to earlier notes remain part of those records until you remove them while editing. They may be included in device sync that you start and in backups you export.
  • Tasks and reviews. The app stores task text, dates, times, repetition and reminder settings, completion history, comments, daily and weekly reviews, and related drafts. You can choose to read external calendars already available on your device. Naoneul leaves the original external events intact.
  • Optional external calendar access. If you grant calendar read permission and select calendars, Naoneul can read calendar names and account display information. It uses event titles, descriptions, locations, start and end times, all-day status, and recurring instances in Today, This Week, the calendar, search, daily reviews, and Ieum. Naoneul does not send this information to a developer server, create a sync account, or require a separate cloud sign-in. The original provider manages your calendar account and synchronization.
  • Connections and analysis. The app processes your connections to people, places, and topics, relationships between records, suggested related material, search indexes, OCR text, photo classification, summaries, and suggestions on your device for search, connections, book organization, and writing. Ieum AI also runs model inference on your device.
  • Drafts and conversations. The app keeps note and task drafts, your current Ieum AI conversation, and the material it used on your device so you can continue your work and view answers.
  • Reminders and follow-ups. The app remembers items it surfaced, links to related notes or tasks, and whether you viewed them. This history is separate from notifications shown by Android and can remain when those notifications are off.
  • Camera, photos, and voice input. Photos you take, scan, or select can become attachments and are used for on-device OCR and image analysis. When you start voice input, the app uses the microphone and requests Android on-device speech recognition. It does not send the audio to a developer server or fall back to network speech recognition. Audio files you choose to attach can be stored with your records.
  • Settings and support. The app uses display, input, notification, analysis, and security settings, backup and restore timestamps, and local diagnostics to provide features and diagnose errors. If you email the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments you send are used to respond and follow up.

2. Permissions and your choices

The app requests permissions such as camera and microphone when you use the relevant feature. You can change permissions and optional settings for link previews, analysis, and notifications in the app or Android settings. You can still type a note if you decline camera or microphone access.

Naoneul schedules and shows task and daily review notifications on your device. You can also enable a notification when Ieum finishes answering. The former note reminder feature has been retired. Changing notification settings is separate from deleting notes, tasks, or follow-up history.

3. External services and transfers

  • Google ML Kit. Naoneul uses it for on-device text recognition, image analysis, document scanning, language detection, and translation. An Ieum AI model installed on the device summarizes text in photos. Downloading translation language models requires an internet connection. The text you translate and the photos and text you summarize are processed on your device. Google may process device and app information, device or installation identifiers, performance metrics, feature usage events, detected languages, and selected translation languages for diagnostics and usage analysis. See ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Optional device sync. When you start a sync with paired nearby Android devices, the app uses Google Play services Nearby Connections to send supported notes and attachments, books, events, tasks, challenges, and their links over an encrypted connection between the devices. This may include original notices attached to events and their zoom locations, Naoneul calendar names, assignments and deletion information (including empty calendars), routine names, step timetables, weekday assignments, changes for specific dates, completed and unchecked steps, and Connections topic names and record links or unlinks. Received information is saved on the other device. It does not pass through the developer's record server. To improve the feature, Google Play services may process metrics such as connection speed, success, and data volume, as well as device model, country, build version, and app package name. You can manage usage and diagnostic settings in your device's Google settings. See Nearby data collection.
  • AI model downloads. The app downloads the model you select from Hugging Face and linked file servers. Those providers may process connection information such as your IP address and the file URL you requested. The download does not send your records, photos, or conversations to the model provider. The downloaded model runs on your device. See the Hugging Face Privacy Policy.
  • Link previews. If enabled, the app may connect to websites you linked and their image servers to retrieve previews. Those operators may receive standard connection information such as IP addresses and requested URLs. Your selected browser or app handles links you open directly.
  • On-device speech recognition setup. A provider may use the network to prepare on-device speech recognition models. Provider policies and device settings govern that processing and retention.
  • Sharing, exports, and opening in other apps. When you choose to share, export, or open content elsewhere, the selected app or storage location receives the applicable record, photo, attachment, or encrypted backup. A selected cloud storage service or app may retain a copy under its own policy.
  • Support. Your chosen email service and the developer's email service transmit and store your inquiry. You may omit sensitive material or records that are unnecessary for the response.

The developer does not sell user records for advertising or collect them on a separate records server. On-device AI runs locally. The external features above may still use a network connection.

4. Storage, protection, and retention

The app encrypts its records database and stored attachments on the device by default. Protection may differ if you disable a developer setting or a change is still in progress. The app displays current protection status. Settings, temporary files used for capture, import, restore, or opening files externally, and copies you export can have different protection. The app attempts to clean up temporary files and can retry if an interruption or error leaves them behind.

  • Saved records, tasks, organization, and links. They stay on your device until you delete them or clear app data. Completed or closed records can remain available to review.
  • Drafts and conversations. They are kept for resuming your work and may be replaced or cleared when you save, clear a draft, start a new conversation, or use the relevant delete feature. They can be retained independently of ordinary records.
  • Follow-up history. Viewed items remain until you clear them. Marking an item as read is separate from completing a task or deleting a record.
  • Indexes, analysis results, and temporary data. These support search, analysis, and display. The app updates and cleans them when source records change or are deleted, and during feature-specific cleanup. If attachment cleanup fails, the app can check whether the file is still in use and retry later.
  • Support emails. Your email may be retained by the email services for as long as needed to reply and follow up. To request deletion, contact the developer below.

Make a backup if you want to recover your records after losing or damaging your device. Android removes internal data according to its policies when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Backups, shared copies, and gallery copies that you saved elsewhere remain in their respective locations.

5. Manual backups and pre-restore recovery copies

Naoneul does not use Android automatic backup. Manual backups are encrypted with a password you choose and exported to a location you select. You manage backup storage, sharing, deletion, and passwords yourself.

  • What manual backups include. Saved notes, events, tasks, challenges, reviews, comments, books, connections, follow-up history, and the related attachments. They can include Naoneul calendars and event assignments, routines and completion states, original notices attached to events and their zoom locations, and drafts of daily reviews in progress.
  • External events. Selected calendar identifiers are stored in app settings on the device. External events are read on demand. The app does not automatically copy them into Naoneul tasks or completed daily records. An encrypted Ieum conversation or a recovery copy can retain event details quoted in answers. You can separately delete the conversation or recovery copy if you disconnect the calendar or revoke permission. The original provider manages original events.
  • What manual backups exclude. Ordinary note and task drafts, current Ieum AI conversations, app settings, external events from the device, and installed AI models. Material you save separately as a record is included with saved records.
  • What a restore replaces. Restoring replaces your current records and attachments with the backup contents. Drafts in progress and your current conversation can also be reset. The app explains the scope and precautions before you restore.
  • Separate copies made before a restore. For recovery, the app may retain the previous database, attachments, conversations, drafts, originals from the camera, and associated protection keys in private app storage. These copies are separate from exported backups and can retain older data after you delete current records.
  • Retention and deletion of recovery copies. After successful verification and restore, the app cleans up prior successful recovery copies so the most recent one remains. Copies isolated after errors or interruptions stay available for recovery. A cleanup error can leave additional copies. The backup screen lets you check storage and delete copies. Current records and exported backups have their own deletion controls.

6. Editing and deleting information

  • You can edit or delete records, tasks, conversations, connections, and follow-up history using the applicable app screens. You can revoke permissions in Android settings.
  • Deleting a book or removing a record from it can differ from deleting the record itself. When clearing a book, records also in another book are removed from the selected book only. The confirmation screen explains what is retained.
  • Deleting a record can leave separate conversation quotes, closed daily records, follow-up history, pre-restore recovery copies, exported backups, and shared copies. Use the relevant screen or storage location to remove those copies.
  • An attachment used by another record or left after a cleanup error may remain. You can check and retry failed cleanup in the app.
  • To remove all internal app data, clear app data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Check synced devices, external storage, your gallery, and other apps for copies saved there.
  • The developer has no record server and cannot remotely access or delete records on your device. To request deletion of information you sent directly to the developer, such as a support email, contact us below.
